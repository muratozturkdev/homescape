# Homescape

Open **Homescape** from the sidebar after starting the add-on. It uses your Home Assistant login, so there is nothing to
configure.

- The first time, Homescape builds a plan from your areas and entities. Switch to **Edit** to draw rooms, move items and
  bind devices.
- Your plan is synced to your Home Assistant user, so every device you sign in with shows the same home.
- The interface follows your Home Assistant language; another language can be picked in Homescape's language menu.

More: https://github.com/muratozturk5/homescape
