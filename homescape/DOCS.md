# Homescape

Open **Homescape** from the sidebar after starting the app. It uses your Home Assistant login, so there is nothing to
configure.

- The first time, Homescape builds a plan from your areas and entities. Switch to **Edit** to draw rooms, move items and
  bind devices.
- Your plan is stored in Home Assistant under your user, so every device you sign in with shows the same home.
- Removing or updating this app does not touch your plan. The app itself stores nothing; Home Assistant backups include
  your plan, and Homescape keeps its own copies under **Plan history** in the home menu.
- The interface follows your Home Assistant language; another language can be picked in Homescape's language menu.

More: https://github.com/muratozturkdev/homescape
