# Homescape

![Homescape showing the sample home in 3D with its rooms, devices and the street](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/muratozturk5/homescape/main/docs/screenshots/overview.png)

Your home as a live 3D dollhouse in the Home Assistant sidebar: lights glow, the TV plays, doors open with their sensors,
and every device can be controlled by clicking it. The first plan is built from your areas and entities; you can shape it
in 3D.
